On hearing from Marty that the hotel room's bathing facilities included only _a mere shower,_ the tiger declared that they were not up to the requirements of a deity performing a holistic cleansing ritual, and offered to take Marty to his own baths.  The sable was initially hesitant - they were comfy, unclothed, and in private - but gave in to his striped benefactor's amused reassurances that clothing would not be necessary for the trip.  "Nobody will even know we've left your room," he reassured.  "Close your eyes and hold on to me, though - this _is_ going to feel a bit strange, and you may not want to see it."

Marty closed his eyes, and felt the tiger shield his face with one paw, as well.  Then, suddenly, gravity went haywire.  Marty felt himself go into free-fall, held in place only by the tiger's arms, and stifled a yelp.  It was brief; as his sense of "down" returned, he felt it swing wildly.  As things stabilized, it seemed that down had changed from _against_ the tiger's chest to _along_ the tiger's chest, as though the big cat were suddenly standing.  As he tried to slow his heart back down, in the background, he could hear that the low hum and gentle whoosh of his hotel room's ventilation had been replaced by the gentle chirping of what sounded like crickets.  The air was warmer, and more humid.  "Wh...whoa.  Is...is it safe to open my eyes, yet?"

The tiger's voice was ripe with amusement.  "Slowly, but yes, you can open your eyes, pet."

Marty peeked out of the eye closest to his tiger's chest, from which he could see that the protective paw still partially shielded his view.  The big cat, face lit by a dim glow from somewhere nearby, was smiling down at him, and seemed to be watching his expression as Marty started to really see.  There was some kind of dappled, textured light coming from above and behind the tiger, which Marty thought looked sort of like stars in a moonless night sky.  He slowly opened his other eye.  The stars were definitely not the only light, or even most of the light.  Gentle, warm luminance seemed to come from all around.  As the tiger slowly removed his paw, Marty gasped.

Most of the light seemed to come from the trees.  They were in a forest, or possibly a garden; the distinction here made little difference.  Blooms with beautiful, luminous, feathery stigma and filaments extending from among their colorful petals, themselves veined with contrasting luminescence, provided most of the light immediately around them.  Closer to the ground, mushrooms and fungi of various shapes and sizes emitted a pleasant glow from beneath their caps, or highlighted trees or stones with emanations directly from their stems or shelves.  Clusters of leaves overhead reflected warm light down toward them, though its source was not readily apparent.  And just ahead of the tiger, in the direction he appeared to be gradually heading, there were gently glowing fish and mollusks moving slowly around a large pool of water, itself ringed with more of the bioluminescent fungi and wreathed in slowly rising steam.  The illuminated leaves in the canopy above filtered the starlight, letting through patches of the brightest view of the milky way that Marty had ever seen.

The sable's eyes were as wide as the tiger's were amused.  "What is this place?" he asked in an awed whisper.

"This," the tiger intoned, "is a _proper_ bath."  There was a quiet gurgling sound from the water's edge as he stepped smoothly in.  "And you, my pet, are about to be _properly_ bathed."

"Is...is it safe?" Marty asked.

"No, I'm afraid not," came the reply.  "I'm told there's a _very large tiger_ in it.  Think it's worth the risk?"  The tiger's grin was the biggest he'd ever seen on any cat.  "I hear they can _bite,"_ he said, punctuating the last word with a snap of his jaws and a chuckle at Marty.

Marty leaned a little harder against the big cat's chest.  "I think I'll take my chances, in _that_ case."

The quiet gurgle of the inverse tiger's black-furred form passing through the water grew gradually louder as he went gradually deeper, and quieted again as he slowed.  "Alright, here you go," he said, as he reached the point in his progression at which he slowly began to dip Marty into the water, as well.

"Ooh.  _Oh wow,_ it's really hot," said the sable.

"Yes.  _Proper._  We'll go slow.  I promise, it won't hurt you," he said, "not unless you find relaxation damaging."

Marty nodded, and tried to steel himself for the transition.  "So is this just...a spot you knew of?" the sable asked.

"Made, sort of," the tiger answered, still holding Marty as he slowly lowered both of them into the water.  "The spring needed a bit of encouragement, the stones some massage.  The creatures in the water seemed happiest here, and seem to like what I bring them, as well as what falls in.  I simply brought them together, and attend to their harmony."

Marty tried to ignore the sensation of the hot springwater lapping over his waist.  "So this is yours?"

"In the sense that I tend to it, yes, I suppose it is."

"How long have you been tending to it?" Marty asked.

The tiger grinned.  "A very, _very_ long time.  We have a rich history together."  He paused to pull Marty slowly away from him, still offering some support, but gently encouraging the smaller Marty to float on his own.  "There, relax - there you go.  See?  You can float comfortably here - just a little farther."

"Do you live here?"  Marty asked, still trying to relax.  The tiger still had a hand under his back, supporting him right in the hollow between his shoulder blades, but it was their last point of contact.

"As nearly as I live anywhere.  I return here when I want somewhere to be myself, and if I find things or creatures that delight me, and I think they might enjoy it too, sometimes, I bring them here."  He let go of Marty completely for a moment, watching the sable struggle a little to relax into the water.  Gently, he placed a fingertip on Marty's forehead.  "Shhh, pet.  Deep breaths.  Let the water carry you, and just relax."  

Marty let himself focus on the gentle contact with his forehead, slowly closed his eyes, and let his body rest in the gently rippling water.  He took a deep breath, let it out slowly.  Took another.  Without opening his eyes, asked, "what kinds of creatures?"

Marty heard as well as felt a quiet purr start from the big cat's chest.  "Well, most recently," the tiger said, and paused, lifting his finger from Marty's forehead and arranging his own body, _"...you."_  At this last, Marty felt a slow and gentle pressure well up underneath him as the tiger's form floated slowly up into contact with his own, their fur mediating the contact.  The sable eventually felt himself lifted slightly out of the water by his protector's buoyancy, his arms resting on the tiger's arms, his head resting in the hollow between his collar bones.  The tiger took a deep breath and let it out slowly, and Marty felt the big cat move with this respiration across his entire body.

Marty felt, for a moment, like he needed to find something else to say, or he risked letting the conversation fall.  But as his tiger took another deep breath beneath him, he realized the moment didn't strictly require any more words.  The two of them were laying, relaxed, together, in the spring, and the moment felt complete in itself; so he released the search for words, and felt it depart from him as though it were sinking into the hot spring beneath the two of them.  Instead, he tried to see if he could make his breathing complement the tiger's, and as their bodies began to move in time with one another, he felt the gentle rumble of the tiger's low, pleased purr swell louder in his own chest.  The cadence was not easy to keep - the tiger's breaths were long and deep, and got longer and deeper the further he relaxed - but the closer he got, the easier it became.  The slow, deep breaths encouraged his own body to relax, and the tiger's purr reverberating through both of them gave the sable such a sense of connection with the god beneath him that with his eyes still closed, he began having trouble feeling where his own body ended.

After a couple of minutes, through the pleasant haze that had enveloped his mind, Marty distantly felt his master stir ever so slightly.  His own left arm (he had to double-check to be sure) slipped slightly lower in the water; the tiger's arm had moved gently out from under it.  He felt a weight on his chest as the big cat placed that paw on Marty's chest and gently pet him with it.  "Sshhh, remain at peace," he heard, in a voice so deep that it was not so much speech as it was modulated purring, still resonating through him.  "You follow my breath so well, my pet."  Another slow, deep breath.  "It's lovely feeling your trust.  Good, keep breathing with me."  Another deep breath.  "I'm right here, pet.  You rest with me, and on me."  Another slow breath.  "Feel me beneath you, little one.  Nothing here will hurt you.  You're safe."  Another deep breath.  "I will not leave you, and nothing can take you from me.  Rest."  Another slow breath.  "Remember this.  In a moment, I need you to walk through your memory with the coyote one last time."  Another deep breath, and this time Marty felt the tiger's paw rub him soothingly again just before he realized he'd tensed up.  "Sshhh, pet, it's okay.  I'll be right here with you the whole time."  Another slow breath.  "He can't hurt you, and this is the last time you need to remember ever again."  Another deep breath.  "I need you to be brave for me.  When you remember, if there are scars from the hurt, they will resonate in your mind."  Another slow breath.  "When they resonate, I can see them; when I see them, I will soothe them."  Another deep breath.  "Remember, I'll be right here with you.  You're _safe._  You'll be safe the whole time."  

Another slow breath.  "Bad things, scary things, cannot withstand the water of this spring.  You will feel the water wash over you as we go."  Another deep breath.  "Let it connect you to me.  Let it remind you that you are here, and safe."  Another slow breath.  "If there is worry, or pain, or fear - the water will carry it away.  Breathe out when you feel the water, and let it carry away the bad things."  Another deep breath, and Marty felt the tiger place something over his nose.  "This will let you breathe when the water comes.  When you feel the water, breathe out."  Another slow breath.  "When you are ready, little one, you may begin."

Marty felt his right arm shift the same way his left had before the tiger had started speaking, and heard ripples off to his right.  _Safe, though,_ he thought.  _Water is safe._  Following his master's cadence, he took another deep breath, this one a little ragged around the edges, and as he let it out, he began.  _Things diverged at the back exit,_ he thought.  _I fell on the far side of the dumpster.  The door opened, the coyote stepped out..._  He felt himself tense, and felt the tiger's purrs from beneath him again.  _Stepped out.  Looked at me, and came around the dumpster.  The drugs started to take consciousness from me..._  He tensed again, heard the ripples again to his right.

"Let the water take the fear, little one."  

As Marty reached the exhalation in his breathing cadence, he felt warm spring water start to run over his face, nose protected, and as the stream passed on down his neck and continued down his chest, he let go of the fear.  He felt the clean water wash it away.  

_He slapped me to make sure I wasn't awake.  He slapped_ hard, _to see if I would fight him.  He didn't care, he just wanted a limp target,_ Marty thought, beginning to get angry.  There was a sound of rippling water again.  As he exhaled, he felt the warmth of the water run over his face.  As it passed down his body again, he let go of the anger.  He felt the clean water wash it away.  He felt himself resting on his master, protecting him.  The coyote could not hurt him; he was safe.  

_There was somebody at the door - he had an accomplice.  They helped him drug me._  He'd been betrayed!  Again, the sound of rippling water came, but this time, it did not come alone.  _They sounded worried,_ he remembered.  _They believed I'd be alright,_ he thought, _that I just wouldn't remember.  They didn't know he'd used too much._  Exhale.  He felt the warm water run over his face.  He forgave the accomplice as it passed down his body again, imagined the betrayal flushed away in little runnels as it sank down into the depths of the spring below.  

_He tore my clothes off, moved the inconvenient parts of me out of the way, and fucked me.  He just barely didn't go in dry, but that spit wasn't enough.  I was barely awake, and I can still remember how much that hurt,_ he thought.  Ripples.  Deep breath.  Exhale.  The warm water running over his face and down his body carried the pain away.  He felt the tiger's paw squeeze against his chest.  _I feel so dirty.  So used,_ he thought.  More ripples.  Slow breath, in time with his master.  His protector.  He was safe here.  Exhale.  He let the warm water take the stains from him as it ran over his face and passed down his body.  Felt the taint run from him, and sink down into the spring below.  

_By the time he finished, I was already dying.  He overdosed me so badly I shouldn't have survived, and there was nothing I could do to stop him._  This time, along with the sound of ripples, he felt a kiss planted on the top of his head.  _Loved,_ he thought.  Deep breath.  He faced the fear.  Loved as he was, there was little that fear could do to him.  As the warm water washed over his face and passed down his body, he let go of the fear, felt the water carry it away.  He'd survived.  He'd been _protected,_ and had survived.

_He tried to dump my body in the dumpster as soon as he was done with me.  I would have died there._  Marty heard the ripples again, and took the deep breath, but this time, he barely needed the water.  _I was found there by people who knew what to do,_ he thought.  _I survived because of that dumpster.  It wasn't a coffin!_  Exhale.  As the water touched his face, he exulted with its warmth.  _I was brought back, cared for._  He felt the water take up his exuberance, but instead of washing it away, he was a fountain.  He had so much more to celebrate, here.  Without thinking about it, as the water passed down his body, he laughed aloud, and heard an answering laugh from the tiger beneath him.  

Marty felt both of the tiger's arms encircle his chest and hug tight.  The big cat shifted their position until they were both upright in the spring, and took Marty by the waist.  "And thus cleansed, releasing the fear and pain into the healing waters, my pet, you are whole once more.  Join me for one last rinse beneath," the tiger intoned.  Marty held his nose as the big tiger dipped both of them beneath the surface, and while they were both beneath, pulled Marty to him and embraced him tightly before letting them both float back up to the surface.  "I'm so proud of you, little one.  You were very brave."

Marty did not have words.  He simply wrapped his arms as far as he could around that huge neck, and hugged back as best he was able as the tiger carried him up and out of the spring on the far side of the basin.
